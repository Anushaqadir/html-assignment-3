Swat Valley - KPK

Called the "Switzerland of Pakistan".

Places:

  • Kalam Valley - Green valleys and rivers
  • Mahodand Lake - Turquoise lake in mountains
  • Malam Jabba - Ski resort
  • Ushu Forest


Naran & Kaghan - KPK

Famous for lush green valleys and lakes.

Places:

  • Saif-ul-Malook Lake - Fairy tale lake
  • Babusar Pass - Gateway to Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Lulusar Lake
  • Shogran


Hunza Valley - Gilgit-Baltistan

Called "Heaven on Earth".

Places:

  • Attabad Lake - Bright blue lake
  • Passu Cones - Sharp mountain peaks
  • Karimabad & Baltit Fort
  • Eagle's Nest - Best sunset view
  • Khunjerab Pass - Highest paved border with China


Skardu - Gilgit-Baltistan

Gateway to K2 and 8000m peaks.

Places:

  • Shangrila Lake - "Heaven on Earth Resort"
  • Upper Kachura Lake
  • Deosai Plains - "Land of Giants", 2nd highest plateau
  • K2 Base Camp


Fairy Meadows - Gilgit-Baltistan

Base camp for Nanga Parbat, 9th highest mountain in the world.

Highlights:

  • Best view of Nanga Parbat at sunrise
  • Camping in pine forests
  • Trekking destination
