Karachi

Places:

  • Clifton Beach: Most famous beach in Pakistan
  • Mohatta Palace: Museum with Mughal + Rajput architecture
  • Quaid-e-Azam Mausoleum: Tomb of Founder of Pakistan
  • Port Grand: Food street by the sea

Thatta

Places:

  • Makli Necropolis: UNESCO World Heritage Site, one of world's largest graveyards
  • Shah Jahan Mosque: Built with 100 domes, famous for blue tile work

Larkana

Places:

  • Mohenjo-daro: 5000 year old city of Indus Valley Civilization. One of the earliest urban settlements
  • Lake Manchar: Largest natural lake in Pakistan

Tharparkar

Places:

  • Thar Desert: Golden sand dunes and colorful culture
  • Nagarparkar: Ancient Jain temples and Karoonjhar mountains
  • Known for rainbow festivals and handicrafts
