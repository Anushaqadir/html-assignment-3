Lahore

Called the "Heart of Pakistan". Famous for Mughal architecture, food, and culture.

Places:

  • Badshahi Mosque - One of the largest mosques in the world
  • Lahore Fort - UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Shalimar Gardens - Mughal Gardens
  • Wagah Border - Daily flag lowering ceremony


Multan

City of Saints and Blue Pottery.

Places:

  • Tomb of Shah Rukn-e-Alam

Capital of Pakistan, located in ICT.
