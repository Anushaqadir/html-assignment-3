Gwadar

Major port city on Arabian Sea.

Places:

  • Gwadar Beach - Turquoise water and clean sand
  • Hammerhead - Rock formation in the sea
  • Gwadar Port - Deep sea port, part of CPEC
  • Koh-e-Batil - Hill with full city + sea view


Hingol National Park

Pakistan's largest national park. Looks like Mars.

Places:

  • Princess of Hope - Natural rock sculpture
  • Sphinx of Balochistan - Rock shaped like a sphinx
  • Mud Volcanoes - Active mud volcanoes
  • Kund Malir Beach - "Desert meets Sea" view
  • Hingol River


Quetta & Ziarat

Provincial capital and hill station.

Places:

  • Hanna Lake - Blue lake surrounded by mountains
  • Urak Valley - Apple and cherry gardens
  • Ziarat - World's largest Juniper forest
  • Quaid-e-Azam Residency - Where Quaid spent last days


Makran Coastal Highway

One of the most scenic coastal roads in the world.

Places:

  • Ormara Beach - Clean fishing town
  • Pasni - Coastal town with mountains
  • Golden Beach - Golden sand + blue sea
  • Mountain + Sea views together
