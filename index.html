Basic Information

General Geography Government Culture Economy

1. General Information

  • Capital: Islamabad
  • Largest City: Karachi
  • Official Languages: Urdu & English
  • Currency: Pakistani Rupee (PKR)
  • Population: ~240+ Million
  • Independence Day: 14th August 1947

2. Geography

  • Location: South Asia
  • Area: 881,913 sq km
  • Provinces: Sindh, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan
  • Territories: Gilgit-Baltistan, Azad Jammu & Kashmir
  • Highest Point: K2 - 8,611m
  • Main River: Indus River

3. Government & System

  • System: Parliamentary Democratic Republic
  • Head of State: President
  • Head of Government: Prime Minister
  • Legislature: Parliament - National Assembly + Senate

4. Culture & People

  • National Flag: Green with white crescent and star
  • National Sport: Hockey
  • National Dress: Shalwar Kameez
  • Famous Food: Biryani, Nihari, Karahi, Chapli Kebab
  • Historical Sites: Mohenjo-daro, Lahore Fort, Badshahi Mosque

5. Economy

  • Main Industries: Textiles, Agriculture, IT, Cement
  • Main Crops: Wheat, Rice, Cotton, Sugarcane
  • Major Cities: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Multan
